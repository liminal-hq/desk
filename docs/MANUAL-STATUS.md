## What is done

Everything in the plan is built; nothing is half-written and no chapter is in progress.

- **W1, the generator** (`manual.py`, `manual_maps.py`, `build.py`, `mdlite.py`, `figures.py`, `plan.json`, the contents section, `manual.html`, the pager, the backlinks on log and anatomy pages, state tags, glossary, commands, sources, staleness and `--manual-report`).
- **All 22 chapters, M1 to M22**, in `tools/progress-report/manual/`, each with the template's sections, a `status` block with evidence, a `try` block whose commands were run, a glossary block and Revisions. Their own checks are in `tools/progress-report/tests/test_chapter_*.py`.
- **All four appendices.** A (glossary), C (commands) and D (sources) are generated from the chapters; B (maps and numbers) is generated by `manual_maps.py` from the files that hold the numbers, with a proof per row.
- **All nine explainers** in `tools/progress-report/manual/explainers/`: `address` (X1), `gdt` (X2), `image` (X3), `frames` (X4), `irq` (X5), `crates` (X6), `dfn` (X7), `targets` (X8), `negatives` (X9).
- **The editorial pass** over first-person status notes. The `reviewed` field of every chapter is `44f0265`, the commit of `main` they were checked against.

Nothing is tagged half-way: where a chapter could not claim more, its record is `built-unverified` or `planned` with the reason in its note.

## What could not be verified

- **Anything on real hardware.** Nothing in the repository has run on a real 486, a real floppy or a real card. Every `built-verified` tag in the Manual means in an emulator or on the host.
- **Bochs and 86Box** were not run for the chapters (their Docker images were not built); M20's results table is copied from `docs/NATIVE-EMULATORS.md`. **js-dos** and the opt-in checks (`INPUT_EXTRA`, `NATIVE_EXTRA`, `JSDOS`) were not run.
- **The DOS side** was run only for M17 and M19 (in the CI image through Docker); the other chapters' DOS statements rest on committed transcripts, and their status notes say so.
- **`tools/check.sh` in full** was not run locally (it needs the DOS toolchain); CI runs it on every pull request, and #40 to #57 passed. #58 to #63 were pending when this was written.
- **Text of history in the sidebars** was checked against the pages cited (fetched), never treated as evidence.

- **No script runs the `bios-solo` test mode**, and no test makes a floppy read fail, so the retry path of stage 1 has never run (both are tagged built-unverified).
- **M11's mouse-packet section** is lighter than its neighbours (M3 decodes a packet already); M17 says little about `bios_kbd`, `env` and `io`.
- **Gallery tiles** for manual figures exist; the planned `thumb: steps` cards show the first step titles only.

## Build and check the Manual

From the root of a checkout of the top branch:

```bash
python3 -I tools/progress-report/tests/test_report.py                                   # every generator and chapter test (239)
python3 -I tools/check-docs.py                                                          # markdown style and links
tools/check-headers.sh                                                                  # licence headers
python3 tools/progress-report/gather.py --offline --out dist/progress-report-data.json  # or without --offline, with gh signed in
python3 tools/progress-report/build.py --data dist/progress-report-data.json --check    # builds the site and checks every page and link
python3 tools/progress-report/build.py --data dist/progress-report-data.json --manual-report   # staleness and downgraded records, as text
python3 -m http.server -d dist/progress-report 8000                                     # then look at manual.html
```

`tools/check.sh` runs the first, second and third as part of its steps. Publishing is the recipe in [`tools/progress-report/README.md`](../tools/progress-report/README.md#publishing).
