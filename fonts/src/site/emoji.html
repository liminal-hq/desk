Desk Emoji

The emoji of Desk, the graphical shell for MS-DOS: original pixel art in the 16-colour Desk palette at 16, 12 and 8 pixels, and flat geometric drawings for Desk Sans, Mono and Serif. Every picture below is drawn from the build's own data, branch feat/emoji.

Contact sheets

Type tester

Set in the real built fonts, embedded in this page. Pixel faces are exact at whole multiples of their size; the colour fonts render through COLR in browsers that support it.

The set

Every emoji is one Unicode code point with its CLDR short name. No ZWJ sequences, skin tones, keycaps or flags. The search box above filters this list too.

Files

  • Windows 3.1 to 95, NT 3.5x, OS/2: the .ttf faces, which stay emoji-free and byte-for-byte unchanged. 322 of the emoji are beyond the Basic Multilingual Plane, which those systems cannot reach.
  • Windows 10 and 11, Linux, macOS, browsers: the .otf faces for monochrome emoji in Desk text; Desk Emoji Pixel or Desk Emoji for colour, listed before the text face.
  • DOS: emoji16.dem, emoji12.dem and emoji8.dem, layered 1-bit bitmaps in the 16 Desk colours.

The zip holds fonts/generated/emoji/ and the SIL Open Font License. Downloading needs the claude.ai viewer; elsewhere the files are in the repository.
